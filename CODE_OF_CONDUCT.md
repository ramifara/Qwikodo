# Code of conduct

Qwikodo is built in public, and everyone participating in the project is expected to help keep it
welcoming, constructive, and safe.

Be respectful. Discuss the work, not the person. Assume good intent, accept correction gracefully,
and make room for people with different experience levels and backgrounds. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not
acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior harms the community. Enforcement decisions should protect affected
people's privacy and be proportionate to the situation.

To report conduct privately, use the repository's private vulnerability-reporting form and mark the
submission as a conduct report. If that form is unavailable, ask a maintainer for a private contact
channel without posting sensitive details.
