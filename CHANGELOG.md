### Added

- Local QR and barcode decoding from files, camera frames, clipboard images, and monitor captures.
- Offline round-trip coverage for 22 ZXing symbologies.
- On-device scan history with copy, delete, and clear controls.
- Minimal Tauri capability and content-security policies.
